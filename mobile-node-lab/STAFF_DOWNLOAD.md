## 📱 Installing on an Android phone

1. Tap **Download TGC Staff Companion** above.
2. Open your phone's **Downloads** and tap the file you downloaded.
3. Tap **Install**. If your phone asks whether to allow installs from your browser or Files app, approve it for this installation.
4. Open **TGC Staff Companion** and follow its setup instructions.
5. **Message Seawolf to get your TGC staff verification code.** Installing the app alone won't unlock access.
6. Enter your code and finish verification in the app. You can then continue setup and sign into VRChat with **your own account** when prompted.

**You won't be able to use the Companion's features until you're verified.**

## 🥽 Installing on a Meta Quest

**Installing the app does not unlock access.** To use the Companion on Quest, **get your TGC staff verification code from Seawolf and complete verification**. You can request the code before or after installing.

### Step 1: Turn on Developer Mode

**You need Developer Mode enabled on your Quest before installing TGC Staff Companion.** Do this first, whichever installation method you use.

**▶️ [Watch this video: How to enable Developer Mode on Quest](https://youtu.be/K3p3P3fCeHo?is=XNw2l6Vs88lLafPl)**

Follow the video and make sure Developer Mode is switched on before continuing. You only need to set it up once unless your Quest settings change.

### Step 2: Install directly on your Quest (recommended)

**You don't need a computer or to open the Quest Browser separately.** Make sure you've enabled Developer Mode using the video above first.

1. On your Quest, open **Discord** and tap the **TGC Staff Companion download-page link** shared by staff.
2. On the page, tap **Download TGC Staff Companion** at the top to download the app directly from the link in Discord.
3. Open **Files** on your Quest, then find the download in **Downloads** or **Recent**.
4. **Do NOT tap the downloaded APK to open it directly.** Instead, find the **three dots (⋯)** next to the downloaded file and select that menu.
5. In that menu, find and choose **Package Installer** (it might be under another option, depending on your Quest). Then tap **Install**.
6. Open your app library and find **TGC Staff Companion**. If it doesn't appear right away, check **Unknown Sources**.

**Important for Quest:** Use the **three dots (⋯) next to the APK** and choose **Package Installer**. **Don't just tap the APK itself.** The exact menu wording may look a little different on your Quest.

**Can't find Package Installer or can't install it this way?** That's okay — follow the computer method below instead.

### Backup option: Install using a computer

If direct installation isn't available on your Quest, you can use **SideQuest** on a computer.

1. Make sure **Developer Mode** is already enabled using the video above.
2. On your computer, click **Download TGC Staff Companion** at the top of this page.
3. Download **[SideQuest](https://sidequestvr.com/)** and open it.
4. Connect your Quest with a USB-C cable that supports file transfers. Put on the headset and allow the connection if asked.
5. In SideQuest, select **Install APK file from folder** and choose the Companion file you downloaded.
6. Back on your Quest, find **TGC Staff Companion** in your app library or **Unknown Sources**.

Once it's installed, open the Companion. **Message Seawolf for your TGC staff verification code** if you haven't already, then enter your code and complete verification. **The app's features remain locked until you are verified.**

> **Need a hand?** Ask the TGC staff team for help. Don't uninstall an older Companion just to try again.

## ✨ What's inside?

The Companion includes useful VRChat tools, staff features, friends and profile tools, avatar height controls, and more. Some optional tools ask for extra permissions or setup; the app will guide you through them.

You don't need to turn on every optional feature to start using the Companion.

## 🔄 Updating the Companion

**Keep the app installed and update it in place.** This helps preserve your saved settings and sign-ins.

To check for an update, open **TGC Staff Companion → Home → App updates & release notes**. You can also find **Check for updates** in the app's Settings.

Only download updates from this official page or from inside the Companion.

## ❓ Having trouble?

- **Can't find the app on Quest?** Check your app library or **Unknown Sources**.
- **Phone won't let you install?** Open the downloaded file from Downloads and follow the permission prompt.
- **Update says “App not installed”?** **Don't uninstall your existing Companion.** Ask TGC staff for help so you don't lose your saved information.
- **Can't use the Companion yet?** Installing it does not grant access to its features. Ask Seawolf for your TGC staff verification code, then complete verification in the app.
- **Something else isn't working?** Open **Setup & troubleshooting** from the Companion's Home screen, or ask the TGC staff team.

**A quick safety reminder:** Never share your VRChat or Discord password, login codes, or saved account information with anyone helping you set up the app.
